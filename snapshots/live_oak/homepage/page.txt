Tips Park in Three Rivers, Texas is closed until further notice due to the flooding - Wednesday, July 29, 2026 @ 6:06 PM
Live Oak County Emergency Management Voluntary Evacuation for low lying areas. Rising waters are threatening neighborhoods prone to flooding such as the Simmons Community area. County officials urge residents in these areas to voluntarily evacuate now or relocate to friends/family outside flood zones. If you live near rivers, creeks, drainage canals, or areas that flood quickly, leave as soon as possible. Take essentials: medications, IDs and important documents, phone chargers, food, water, clothing, and supplies for infants, the elderly, and pets. Secure your home by moving valuables to higher places and unplugging nonessential electrical devices. DO NOT DRIVE THROUGH FLOODED ROADS — six inches of moving water can knock a person down; one foot can carry a car away. - Wednesday, July 29, 2026 @ 9:32 AM
