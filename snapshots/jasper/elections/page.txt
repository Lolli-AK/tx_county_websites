Notice: January 23, 2026
Due to an issue with the Texas Secretary of State's system, there will be a delay in mailing out new Voter Registration Certificates.
Once they have resolved the issue, new certificates will be mailed. Please check your Voting Precinct number due to changes in redistricting.
Thank you for your patience and understanding.
If you have not received your new Voter Registration Certificate before the next election, you can still vote by presenting any of the seven (7) acceptable photo IDs allowed by law.
Texas Driver License
Texas Election ID Certificate
Texas Personal ID Card
Texas Handgun License
U.S. Citizenship Certificate with Photo
U.S. Military ID Card
U.S. Passport (Book or Card)
Holly Thomas, Jasper County Clerk
