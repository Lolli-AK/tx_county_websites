CURRENT ELECTION INFORMATION

Welcome to the new  Kaufman County Elections page.  Here you will find everything you need to navigate the voting process in Kaufman County.  Information is updated as each election approaches.  Simply click on the "Current Election Information" tab on the left in the dark blue column to find what you need to be Election Savvy.

Are You Registered to Vote?

To be eligible to vote in the November 3 , 2026, Gubernatorial/Joint  Election , you must be registered by October 5, 2026 ! Read more .
