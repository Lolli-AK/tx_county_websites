REGISTER TO VOTE
To be eligi
ble to vote in Texas you must be registered at least 30 days before an election.
You can check your registration status by clicking this link:
AM I REGISTERED?
(sos.state.tx.us website)
Voter Registration
Please contact Kaufman County Elections Department at 469-376-4595 should you have any questions regarding your voter registration certificate.
To be eligible to register in Texas, you must:
Be a U.S. citizen
Be a resident of the county
Be 18 years old
Not be a convicted felon (unless a person's sentence is completed, including any probation or parole)
Not declared mentally incapacitated by a court of law
Important note:
To be eligible to vote in any election, you must be registered 30 days prior to Election Day.

Address Changes
Please ensure our office has the most up-to-date information. It is essential that you keep your voter information current so that you may vote in the correct precinct and elections.
Online Address Changes
If you have moved within the county, you may change your name or address online.
Voter name and address change form is available online and is provided by the Secretary of State.
In-Person Address Change
Address or name changes may be made in our office at 3003 S Washington Street in Kaufman, TX, 75142, or at the Department of Public Safety (DPS) office when renewing your driver's license.
If you have your voter certificate, sign your name and fill out the spaces provided with your new address. Mail the certificate to the following address:
Kaufman County Elections Department
P.O. Box 1347
Kaufman, TX 75142
If you do not have your voter certificate, you may access the
Voter Registration Application
online to request a new one.
Attention College Students
Students have two options for which elections they want to participate in, and how they would like to vote.
Students may wish to claim your school/dorm as their residence address. If doing so the student must
register in the county
in which they will be residing. They may then participate in elections under that political jurisdiction.
Register at Home: If your home address is in the state of Texas, you may
use the online Voter Registration Form
, or use the links to request a form be mailed to you.
