Rusk County, Texas
is known for its rich East Texas heritage, historic communities, and lasting contributions to the state's oil and railroad history. Anchored by the City of Henderson, the county is home to portions of the renowned Texas State Railroad and reflects a proud legacy shaped by the East Texas Oil Field. Surrounded by the beauty of the Piney Woods, Rusk County offers a unique blend of history, outdoor recreation, and small-town character that continues to define life in East Texas.
