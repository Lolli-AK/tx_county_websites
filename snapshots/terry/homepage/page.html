County News and Information

Welcome to Terry County, Texas! Located in the heart of the Texas South Plains, Terry County is proud of its rich agricultural heritage, welcoming communities, and strong hometown spirit. Whether you are a longtime resident, new neighbor, or visitor, we invite you to explore our website and discover the people, services, and opportunities that make Terry County a great place to call home.

From thriving agriculture and local businesses to community events, schools, and public services, Terry County continues to grow while preserving the traditions and values that have shaped our communities. We are pleased to provide this website as a convenient resource for residents, businesses, and visitors.
