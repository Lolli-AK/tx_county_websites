Vote with a Ballot by Mail
Who Can Vote by Mail?
You may qualify to vote by mail if you:
Will be age 65 or older on Election Day;
Have a sickness or physical condition that prevents you from voting in person without likely needing personal assistance or risking your health;
Expect to give birth within three weeks before or after Election Day;
Will be outside your county of registration during early voting and on Election Day;
Are civilly committed under Chapter 841 of the Texas Health and Safety Code; or
Are in jail but remain eligible to vote.
Read the Texas voting-by-mail eligibility requirements
.
Request a Ballot by Mail
Call
432-264-2273
to request an application, or visit the
Texas ballot-by-mail application and instructions
.
For the November 3, 2026 election, your application must be received by October 23, 2026, before the close of regular business or noon, whichever is later.
A postmark alone does not meet this application deadline.
Voters applying because of age or disability may request an annual application covering county elections that calendar year. Renew your annual application each year.
Follow the application instructions for identification numbers, signatures, and delivery. If you submit a signed application by email or fax, the original must also reach the early voting clerk within four business days after the emailed or faxed application is received.

Curbside Voting Assistance
A specially marked curbside voting parking space is available at the Election Center during early voting.
How to Request Assistance
Park in the specially marked curbside voting space.
Call the phone number posted on the sign at that location.
Tell the election worker you are requesting curbside voting.
For questions before your visit, call Howard County Elections at
432-264-2273
.
Using Our Voting Equipment
Howard County uses the same equipment at the Election Center during early voting and at every Election Day location: ES&S ExpressPoll tablets, ExpressVote ballot marking devices, and DS200 ballot scanners.
Check In
An election worker will use a check-in tablet to locate your voter record and guide you through identification and check-in. The tablet is used for check-in; you make your ballot selections on the ExpressVote.
Make Your Selections
Follow the election worker's directions to start your ballot on the ExpressVote. Use the touch screen or available accessible controls to make your choices. Review the on-screen summary and make any changes before printing.
Print and Review
Follow the screen prompts to print your ballot. Carefully review the printed selections. If anything is incorrect, ask an election worker for help before scanning it.
Printing your ballot does not cast your vote.
Scan Your Ballot
Insert your reviewed ballot into the DS200 scanner. Wait for confirmation that it has been accepted. If the scanner returns your ballot or displays a message you do not understand, ask an election worker for help before leaving.
Accessible Voting Features
ExpressVote supports larger text, high-contrast display settings, audio ballot instructions, and a tactile keypad. Ask an election worker to help you use these features or review your printed ballot.
Questions before your visit? Call
Howard County Elections at 432-264-2273
.

Information for Candidates & Campaign Finance Reports
Texas Ethics Commission - Reports for Candidates & Office Holders of Howard County
Local Filers
File a campaign treasurer appointment
Form and Instructions Treasurer Appointments (TA) Page
with your local filing authority before you file an application for a place on the ballot, announce your intent to run for public office, or solicit or accept contributions or make expenditures.
If you plan to accept contributions, open a separate bank account that is separate from any other bank account you maintain.
Begin campaigning for elective office and obtain a place on the ballot. (Contact the Texas Secretary of State or your local elections official for information on ballot access.)
Check the filing deadlines and timely file all required campaign finance reports, which may include: Report is due 15 days after filing Campaign Treasurer Appoint (if current office holder)
Semiannual reports due on January 15 of each year;
Semiannual reports due on July 15 of each year;
30-day pre-election reports;
8-day pre-election reports; and
Runoff reports.
Continue filing reports until you file a final report when you expect to have no reportable activity connected with your candidacy. (See Checklist for Ending my Campaign.)
