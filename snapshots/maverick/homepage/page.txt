FEMA Representatives on Site at Alfonso Mallen Recreation Center
1904 Antonio Garcia Street
FEMA's Individual Assistance program is now available to communities affected by this summer's July flooding. Maverick County and Eagle Pass residents may qualify for this relief program.
FEMA representatives will be on site at the Alfonso Mallen Recreation Center (1904 Antonio Garcia Street) on November 24 and 25, 2026, from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. to answer questions and assist those who qualify.
Residents can also apply at disasterassistance.gov, call 800-621-3362, or apply through the FEMA app. The deadline to submit an application is November 29.
When: November 24-25, 2026, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Where: Alfonso Mallen Recreation Center, 1904 Antonio Garcia Street
