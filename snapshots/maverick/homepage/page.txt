Eagle Pass MPO Public Workshop: 2050 Transportation Plan (MTP) & TIP
Wednesday, October 21, 2026 · 5:00 PM
International Center for Trade (ICT), 3295 Bob Rogers Drive, Eagle Pass, TX 78852
Maverick County encourages residents to attend the public workshop hosted by the Eagle Pass Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) on the 2050 Metropolitan Transportation Plan (MTP) and the Transportation Improvement Program (TIP).
Learn about upcoming transportation plans and projects, share your ideas, and highlight the transportation needs that matter most to you and your family. Whether you drive, walk, bike, or use public transportation, your perspective is important.
For questions or additional information, or to arrange free translation services or disability assistance at least three business days in advance: sfigueroa@eaglepasstx.gov or (830) 773-4247.
