Current Elections

The next election in Erath County is the General Election November 3, 2026.


Important Dates:

Last day to register to vote for this election: Monday, October 5, 2026

Last day to submit an application to vote by mail in person or by mail to the Elections Administration Office: Friday, October 23, 2026.


Election Day will be Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (7:00am - 7:00pm)
