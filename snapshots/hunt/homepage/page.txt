Hunt County, Texas
is defined by its rich Blackland Prairie landscape, historic roots, and continued growth as part of the North Texas region. Anchored by Greenville, the county blends a strong agricultural foundation with expanding industry and economic development, including a notable presence in aerospace and defense. Supported by its proximity to the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, Hunt County offers a balance of small-town charm, regional connectivity, and opportunity.
