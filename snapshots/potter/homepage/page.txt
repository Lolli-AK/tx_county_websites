***Special Attention***
PARKING - Due to the demolition of the Old District Courts Building at 5th and Fillmore, parking in the adjacent parking lot is prohibited. As a result, we anticipate that downtown parking will be a challenge for the next year. To help you navigate this change, please refer to the map below for areas that are permissible for parking. I encourage everyone to plan ahead when coming to the Courthouses for business or for Jury Duty. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your understanding as we navigate through these changes. Thank you for your attention, and please do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.

Welcome to Potter County
The Texas Panhandle's central region is home to Potter County. It is 581,357 acres, or 908.37 square miles, in total land area. The estimated population of Potter County is 118,015 (2022) and is concentrated in Amarillo and the nearby subdivisions. Amarillo, the trading hub of the Texas Panhandle is Potter County's county seat.
Welcome to Potter County we take pride in making our County a place where people and businesses can thrive and grow.
