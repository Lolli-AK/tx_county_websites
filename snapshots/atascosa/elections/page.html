Campaign Finance

As of July 1, 2025

Campaign Finance Portal – Candidates and Elected Officials now file all their reports to the Election Office online.  First, each individual must create an account at this website: Candidate Filings Once you have an account you can access the different forms from the Texas Ethics Commission – step by step directions click here

Candidate or Elected Official must fill out each form and return to our office:
Form 1 Form 2

Individuals can look at each report that has been filed by each candidate.  To see the reports, you do not have to create an account, just click on the top right of the screen on the word Candidate Filings
