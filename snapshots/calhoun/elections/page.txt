Accessibility Statement
We want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who rely on assistive technology. Accessibility is an ongoing part of how we maintain it.
What we aim for
We aim to conform to the
Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)
2.2 at Level AA. WCAG is the widely used standard for making web content usable by people with disabilities. Level AA is the level most organizations and regulations reference.
Conformance status
This site is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not yet fully meet the standard. The known limitations below say where.
What we do
Pages are checked regularly with automated accessibility testing, and issues are reviewed and corrected as we find them.
Where an issue has a suggested fix, a person reviews it before it is applied to the site.
An accessibility menu on every page lets visitors adjust how the site is presented: text size and spacing, contrast and color, motion, reading aids, and read-aloud. Open it with the accessibility button on the page.
How we assess accessibility
We assess this site through regular automated testing and review by the people who maintain it. What we find is tracked and corrected over time.
Known limitations
Automated testing and the accessibility menu do not catch everything. Some content may still be hard to use, including:
Content from third parties, such as embedded videos, maps, forms, or social media, which we do not control.
Older documents and media that may be missing captions, transcripts, or text alternatives.
Pages that have changed since they were last checked.
If something is in the way, tell us and we will work on it.
Tell us about a problem
If you have trouble using any part of this site, please contact us through the site's usual contact channels and describe what happened, where, and what you were trying to do. We aim to reply within 5 business days and will look for a way to help you get what you need.
If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can ask us to review it. Depending on where you live, you may also be able to raise the matter with a national body that oversees digital accessibility.
Compatibility
This site is designed to work with current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge, and with screen readers including NVDA and JAWS on Windows, VoiceOver on macOS and iOS, and TalkBack on Android. It relies on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WAI-ARIA.
About this statement
This statement was last reviewed in November 2026. We review it as the site changes.
