MAY 26 TH , 2026 JOINT PRIMARY RUNOFF

Notice of Election
Plan of Action
Polling Locations
Deadline to registered April 27 th , 2026
Deadline to request Ballot By Mail will be May 15 th , must be received in our office by 5:00 p.m.
Early Voting will be May 18 th to May 22 nd , 2026 open 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
Early Voting will be held at Angelina County Elections Administration Office (downstairs in office lobby)
606 E Lufkin Ave, Lufkin TX  75901
Election Day we will have the 21 Vote Centers open on May 26 th , 2026 7:00 a.m. To 7:00 p.m.

Applications for ballot by mail shall be mailed to:
(Las solicitudes para boletas de votarán adelantada por correo deberán enviarse a:)

ANGELINA County Early Voting Clerk
(Secretario de la Votación Adelantada del condado de ANGELINA)

Terri Jordan, Elections Administrator (Administradora de las Elecciones)
P.O. Box 1343
Lufkin, TX 75902

Fax: 936-671-5186
Email: tjordan@angelinacounty.net
elections@angelinacounty.net

Applications for Ballot by Mail (ABBM) and Federal Postcard Applications (FPCA), must be received no later than the close of business on May 15, 2026 .
(Las solicitudes de boleta por correo (ABBM) y la tarjeta federal postal de Solicitud (FPCA) deberán recibirse más tardar de las horas de negocio el 15 de mayo del 2026.)

Note: If ABBM is faxed or emailed or if an FPCA is faxed; the original application must be received within 4 business days.
(Si ABBM se envía por fax o correo electrónico o si se envía por fax una FPCA; La solicitud original debe ser recibida dentro de los 4 días hábiles.)
