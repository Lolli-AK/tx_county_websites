Welcome to Lavaca County, Texas
Lavaca County, Texas, nestled in the heart of the state's southeast region, is a charming blend of small-town life, rich history, and rural beauty. Founded in 1846 and named after the Lavaca River, the county offers a glimpse into Texas' storied past with its historic courthouses, classic town squares, and landmarks that date back to the 19th century. The area is known for its strong Czech and German heritage, visible in local architecture, cuisine, and cultural festivals. Agriculture, particularly cattle ranching and farming, plays a vital role in the county's economy. Visitors and locals alike enjoy the slower pace, scenic landscapes of rolling hills, and the sense of close-knit community that defines Lavaca County. It’s a place where Texas traditions meet a peaceful way of life, making it a hidden gem for those seeking an authentic rural Texas experience.

Property tax rates in County of Lavaca.
This notice concerns the 2025 property tax rates for County of Lavaca. This notice provides
information about two tax rates used in adopting the current tax year's tax rate. The no-new-revenue
tax rate would Impose the same amount of taxes as last year if you compare properties taxed in both
years. In most cases, the voter-approval tax rate is the highest tax rate a taxing unit can adopt without
holding an election. In each case, these rates are calculated by dividing the total amount of taxes by
the current taxable value with adjustments as required by state law. The rates are given per $100 of
property value.
This year's no-new-revenue tax rate $0.5124/$100
This year's voter-approval tax rate $0.5497/$100
