Tax Rate and Budget Notices
Brazos County adopted a Tax Rate that will raise more taxes for maintenance and operations than last year’s rate.
The Tax Rate will effectively be raised by 2.17 percent and will raise taxes for maintenance and operations on a $100,000 home by approximately $15.55.
