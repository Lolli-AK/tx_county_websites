  2. Texas Ave. Traffic Alert

    Starting September 7, the outside southbound lane of Texas Avenue will be closed between 27th and 28th streets as the Brazos County Administration Building remodel continues along that side of the building. See a map of the closure...

  3. Notice of Public Hearing on Tax Increase

    A Public Hearing on the Proposed Tax Rate will be held on Sept. 1, 2026 at 9:15 a.m. in the Brazos County Commissioners Courtroom, 200 S. Texas Ave., Bryan, TX 77803. See the notice...

  4. Watch: Presentation on Factors Affecting Proposed Tax Rates

    Determining how to set a Brazos County tax rate each year is quite an involved process. County Auditor Marci Turner presented to commissioners the factors involved and how different rates would affect Maintenance and Operations (M&O) in the future. Watch the presentation...

  5. Notice of Public Hearing on County Clerk Archival Fund

    Commissioners Court will hold a public hearing to consider a plan for the County Clerk Records Management and Archival Fund on Aug. 4, 2026 at 9 a.m. at the Brazos County Administration Building, 200 S. Texas Ave., Suite 106, Bryan, TX 77803. Read the full notice...

  6. View Agendas and Minutes: New Web Portal

    Starting with the July 7, 2026 Commissioners Court meetings, agendas and minutes will be in our new Agenda Management System. You can still view agendas and minutes from past meetings in our Laserfiche Archives and our old system while still available. View agendas and minutes...

  7. Notice of Consideration of Approval of an Amendment to Tax Abatement Agreement

    The Commissioners Court will consider the approval of an amendment concerning an assignment of a tax abatement agreement entered into with RELLIS Campus Data and Research Center, LLC, originally approved on April 29, 2025, at 10 a.m. on Aug. 18, 2026. Read the notice...

  8. Stay Updated on the New World Screwworm

    As stories continue to emerge regarding the New World Screwworm, Brazos County encourages each of you to stay apprised of the latest information via the Texas A&M AgriLife Extension Service. Get latest updates...

  9. Brazos County Completes Employee Compensation and Benefits Study

    The study evaluated employee compensation using objective market data and comparisons to similar organizations. The purpose was simple: ensure that Brazos County remains a competitive employer while being responsible stewards of taxpayer resources. Read more about the study...

  10. Aquifers 101: Let's Talk About Water

    Have questions about our water supply? Worried about data centers coming to our area? Then come hear Alan Day, General Manager of the Brazos Valley Groundwater Conservation District, discuss these topics and more at the Brazos Center on June 30 at 6 p.m. Read more...

  11. Judge Peters Issues Disaster Declaration in Advance of Winter Storm

    This declaration will assist in accessing state resources, if needed, to alleviate emergency situations caused by the potential ice storm. Read about the declaration...

  12. Auditor's Office Honored for Financial Reporting Excellence

    The Government Finance Officers Association of the United States and Canada (GFOA) has awarded the Certificate of Achievement for Excellence in Financial Reporting to Brazos County for its annual comprehensive financial report. Read the news release...

  13. Commissioners Approve Additional Funds for Food Bank

    In response to the federal government shutdown and its effect on those receiving benefits to combat food insecurity, the Brazos County Commissioners have unanimously approved providing an additional $25,000 to the Brazos Valley Food Bank. Read the full release...

  14. New Meeting Dates for Brazos County Commissioners Court

    Beginning in October 2025, the Brazos County Commissioners Court will have regularly scheduled meetings twice a month instead of once a week. View the Schedule...
