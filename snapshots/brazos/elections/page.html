Voting in Brazos County is simple, and your vote counts!

Here you will find information on what to expect , where to vote , how to register to vote, and information on the technological enhancements implemented in accordance with HR 3295 – Help America Vote Act of 2002 in order to make sure your vote is counted accurately and securely.

Also, learn about current issues and how your vote really makes a difference, especially in local and county government elections.
