NOTICE OF ELECTION PRECINCT BOUNDARY CHANGE 2026
The Bastrop County Elections Administrator conducts Federal, State, and County elections. The Elections Administrator also conducts
elections for other governmental entities that may contract with the county for election services.
We hope that you find our website helpful, informative, and easy to navigate. This website is full of information necessary to exercise your right to vote. You should easily be able to find information on how to register to vote, where to vote, and when to vote.
