ARMSTRONG COUNTY FIRE DEPARTMENT

PO Box 523, Claude, TX  79019 / 806-553-2862 / armstrongfire@co.armstrong.tx.us

Effective January 1, 2025, the Armstrong County Fire Department (ACFD) was officially established to centralize and enhance fire protection services within the county. Under the direction of the County, ACFD assumed management of both the Claude and Washburn Volunteer Fire Departments (VFDs), aligning them under a unified county fire service. Commissioner Joe Neely has been appointed as the Fire Director for ACFD, overseeing operations and strategic direction for the department. Each station — Claude and Washburn — continues to operate with its own designated Fire Chief to maintain leadership and operational efficiency at the local level.  Like most Texas Volunteer Fire Departments, Armstrong County Fire Department relies on donations to operate. Your donations are greatly appreciated!

Armstrong County Launches New Emergency Notification System

Armstrong County Emergency Management is announcing a transition to a new mass notification system designed to enhance public safety and streamline emergency communications.

The County is now using WENS (Wireless Emergency Notification System) to provide timely alerts and critical information to residents during emergencies, severe weather events, road closures, and other important situations.

When you are signing up, be sure to select the two additional boxes - Armstrong County Emergency Alerts & Armstrong County Community Notices - to stay informed.

CodeRED or i-INFO will no longer be used. for Armstrong County, effective 10/1/2025.

Be sure to register for WENS to continue receiving emergency alerts and community updates. Sign up now by visiting: https://entry.inspironlogistics.com/armstrong_tx/wens.cfm

The WENS system allows users to receive alerts via text message, phone call, or email, depending on their preference. It is FREE to register, and your information will remain confidential.

Armstrong County Emergency Management Coordinator, Bobby Wilkerson, stated: “Keeping our community safe and informed is our top priority. WENS gives us a reliable, flexible way to reach residents quickly when it matters most. We encourage everyone to take just a minute to sign up.”

For more information or assistance with signing up, please contact Armstrong County Emergency Management at 806-731-1823 or bobby.wilkerson@co.armstrong.tx.us.
