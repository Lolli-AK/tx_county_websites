  1. Am I registered to Vote

    Check to see if you are registered to vote.

  2. Name & Address Change

    Promptly notify our office in writing of a name change. You will need to correct the information on your current voter registration certificate and return to us.

  3. Register to Vote

    You may register to vote at any time. You will need to complete a voter registration application and either mail or bring the application to the office.

  4. Voter Registration Certificate

    You will receive your voter registration certificate within 30 days from the date our office receives your voter registration application.
