How to Register
You may register to vote at any time. You will need to complete a voter registration application and either mail or bring the application to the office. You may pick up an application at either office location or print one online.
Voter Registration Application (PDF)
Voter Registration Spanish (PDF)
Review the following for information on how to register:
College students may register in the county of their permanent residence or in the county where they are going to college
You must be at least 17 years and 10 months of age on the date your application is submitted
Your application must be submitted 30 days before an election for you to be eligible to vote in that election
Your spouse, parent or child (acting as an agent) may complete and sign a voter registration application for you, provided this person is a registered voter, or has applied for voter registration
Incomplete Information
If any of the information on the application is incomplete, you will be notified and required to send a second application. The second application must be received within 10 days to be effective for that election.
