Montague County residents can continue to vote at any vote center.
What does this mean to the voter?
A Vote Center is a Countywide polling location where any registered
voter may cast their vote. Vote Centers offer all electronic voting and
eliminate the Election Day requirement of having to vote in the polling
location specific to your voting precinct. With Vote Centers, a voter
can chose the most convenience polling location in the County during
Early Voting and Election Day. Locations are listed below with the
addresses.
