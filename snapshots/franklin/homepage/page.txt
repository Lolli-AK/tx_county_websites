**BURN BAN REINSTATED – FRANKLIN COUNTY**
The **Franklin County Commissioners Court has reinstated the county-wide burn ban, effective immediately.**
Due to current dry conditions and increased fire danger, residents are urged to comply with the burn ban to help protect lives and property.
**VIOLATIONS MAY RESULT IN FINES OF UP TO $500**
Pursuant to **Texas Local Government Code § 352.081**, a person who knowingly or intentionally violates a county burn ban order commits a Class C misdemeanor, punishable by a fine of up to $500.
We will continue to closely monitor weather and drought conditions and take appropriate action as conditions change.
Thank you for your cooperation in helping keep Franklin County safe!
