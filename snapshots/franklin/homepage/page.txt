BURN BAN LIFTED – EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY
??
Franklin County Judge Scott Lee has officially rescinded the countywide Burn Ban, effective immediately.
The Burn Ban will be discussed at the upcoming Commissioners Court meeting on
Monday, November 9, 2026
, at which time the Court may consider reinstating it depending on weather and drought conditions.
As always, Franklin County officials will continue to closely monitor conditions and take appropriate action as necessary to protect the safety of our residents and property.
We encourage everyone to continue exercising caution when burning outdoors, as conditions can change rapidly.
Thank you for your cooperation and understanding!
