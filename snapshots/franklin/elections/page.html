MISSION STATEMENT

The mission of the Franklin County Elections Department is to provide fair, transparent, and efficient election services to all eligible voters in our community. We are dedicated to conducting secure, accurate, and accessible elections that uphold the democratic process, encourage voter participation, and ensure confidence in the electoral system. Our goal is to serve all voters with integrity and professionalism while continually improving election operations and fostering public trust.
