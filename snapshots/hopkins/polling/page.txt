Rachel Pearson
Elections Administrator
Welcome to the Hopkins County Elections Office! I am honored to serve as the Elections Administrator for Hopkins County.
My goal is to ensure that every election is conducted with
integrity, accuracy, transparency, and professionalism
, while providing every voter with helpful, respectful, and accessible service.
As Elections Administrator, I oversee voter registration, early voting and Election Day operations, election equipment and testing, election worker training, ballot-by-mail processes, and the many behind-the-scenes responsibilities required to conduct secure and successful elections.
I am committed to continuing my education in Texas election law and election administration so that our office remains informed, prepared, and ready to serve our community.
Most importantly, I want the Hopkins County Elections Office to be a place where voters feel welcome and comfortable asking questions. Whether you are registering to vote, requesting a ballot by mail, looking for your polling location, or simply have a question about the election process,
we are here to help.
Serving Hopkins County voters with integrity, accuracy, and transparency.
Leora Bouyssou
Assistant Elections Administrator
Leora Bouyssou serves as the Assistant Elections Administrator for the Hopkins County Elections Office, working alongside the Elections Administrator to help ensure elections are conducted accurately, efficiently, and in accordance with Texas election law.
In her role, Leora assists with voter registration, election preparation, early voting and Election Day operations, election worker support, ballot-by-mail processes, and the day-to-day responsibilities of the Elections Office.
Leora is committed to providing friendly, professional, and dependable service to the voters of Hopkins County. She works to make sure voters have the information and assistance they need while helping our office maintain an organized and successful election process.
She is proud to serve the Hopkins County community and be part of an office dedicated to
integrity, accuracy, transparency, and excellent voter service.
Here to serve. Here to help. Every voter, every election.

Public Information Requests
The Hopkins County Elections Office is committed to transparency and providing access to public records in accordance with the
Texas Public Information Act
.
Request Public Information
To request records from the Hopkins County Elections Office, please complete the
Public Information Request Form
below and submit the completed form to our office.
Public Information Request Form
Please provide as much detail as possible regarding the records you are requesting. Including specific dates, election names, document types, or other identifying information will help our office locate the requested records.
Submit Your Completed Form
Completed Public Information Request Forms may be submitted to:
Hopkins County Elections Office
119 College Street
Sulphur Springs, Texas
Phone:
903-438-3037
You may also submit the completed form by email to:
elections@hopkinscountytx.org
Please Note
Some election and voter registration information is confidential or protected from public disclosure under state or federal law.
Fees may apply for copies, labor, electronic records, or other services as permitted by law. If applicable, our office will provide information regarding estimated charges before processing the request.
For questions regarding a Public Information Request, please contact the Hopkins County Elections Office.
