Voter Registration Information
▲
Do You Need to Register to Vote?
To vote in Texas, you must be registered. If you need to register to vote, you may obtain a Voter Registration Application by using one of the following options:
Download and print the Texas Voter Registration Application (PDF)
Call the Hopkins County Elections Office at
903-438-3036
Pick up an application at locations throughout Hopkins County, including post offices, libraries, and Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) offices
Submit Your Application
Completed applications may be mailed or hand-delivered to:
Hopkins County Elections
119 College Street
Sulphur Springs, TX 75482
You may also fax your application to
903-558-2028
. If you fax your application, the original hard copy must be received by our office within four business days. Please write
“Application was faxed”
at the top of the application.
Renewing Your Driver’s License Online?
When renewing your driver’s license through the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) website, you may be provided a link to update your voter registration information. Please note that you must complete the form, print it, and mail it to the Hopkins County Elections Office.
Who Can Register to Vote?
You are eligible to register to vote if you:
Are a United States citizen
Are a resident of Hopkins County
Are at least 17 years and 10 months of age
Are not a convicted felon (unless you have completed your sentence, including any incarceration, probation, or parole)
Have not been determined by a court exercising probate jurisdiction to be totally mentally incapacitated or partially mentally incapacitated without the right to vote
Important Information
To vote in an election, you must be registered to vote. Your voter registration application must be submitted at least
30 days before an election date
.
In Texas, voters do
not
register by political party. Voters register in the county where they currently reside.
