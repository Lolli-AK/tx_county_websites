Information, Links, and Resources
Quick Access to Online Services
Culberson County, Texas is located in the Trans-Pecos region of West Texas and is known for its expansive desert landscapes and striking mountain scenery.
The town of Van Horn serves as the county seat and a central hub for the area. Culberson County includes much of Guadalupe Mountains National Park, home to Guadalupe Peak, the highest point in Texas, offering opportunities for hiking, outdoor exploration, and scenic views. With wide-open spaces, low population density, and a landscape unlike anywhere else in the state, Culberson County offers a distinctive West Texas setting for residents and visitors alike.
