Welcome to  the Wood County Election/Voter Registration Website!
ATTENTION VOTERS:
WOOD COUNTY COMMISSIONERS COURT APPROVED AND ADOPTED A NEW VOTING SYSTEM ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2023.  WE ARE NOW USING THE HART VERITY SYSTEM, WHICH IS HAND MARKED PAPER BALLOTS WITH VERITY SCANNERS. THERE WILL BE A VERITY DUO, ADA VOTING MACHINE AT EACH POLLING LOCATION AND ANY VOTER THAT WISHES MAY USE THIS DEVICE.
ELECTIONS/VOTER REGISTRATION
OFFICE HOURS are 8 am – 4 pm
Walk In – 200 W. Bermuda St. Quitman, TX 75783
Mailing Address – P.O. Box 970 Quitman, TX 75783
