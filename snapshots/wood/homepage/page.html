  • Welcome to Wood County Wood County, Texas, is a hidden gem nestled in the heart of East Texas, offering the perfect blend of natural beauty, small-town charm, and a thriving community spirit. Known for its picturesque landscapes, including the lush piney woods and sparkling lakes like Lake Fork and Lake Holbrook, it’s a paradise for outdoor enthusiasts and anglers. Beyond its natural allure, Wood County boasts vibrant towns like Quitman, Mineola, and Winnsboro, rich in history, art, and culture, with lively farmer’s markets, festivals, and unique local businesses. Whether you're seeking a serene retreat or a welcoming place to call home, Wood County offers a high quality of life, affordable living, and a sense of belonging that’s hard to find elsewhere.
