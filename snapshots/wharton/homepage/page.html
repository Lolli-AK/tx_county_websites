Pipelines and Utilities
Pipelines and other underground utility lines crisscross our yards, neighborhoods and community. Because we can't see them, we seldom think about their location, potential hazards and tips for keeping them safe. Visit the Texas Pipeline Awareness Alliance Website at http://pipeline-safety.org to learn more about the pipelines near your home or business.
