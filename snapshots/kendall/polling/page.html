A law passed in the 86th Regular Legislative Session (2019 - Senate Bill 902). This bill requires that information on the roster for a person who votes either in person or by mail must be made available for public inspection not later than 11 am on the day after the voter votes or the clerk receives the ballot voted by mail. House Bill 1850 (also passed in the 86th Regular Legislative Session) requires those rosters to be posted on the county's website.
