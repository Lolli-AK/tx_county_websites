Volunteer Deputy Voter Registrars
In order to encourage voter registration, Texas election law provides for County Voter Registrars to designate individuals to assist in the registration of eligible persons to vote.
Voter ID Requirements
Senate Bill 5 requires voters to present identification in order to vote in person in all Texas elections.
Voter Registration Information
To be eligible for voter registration, applicants must be a U.S. citizen, a resident of the county, 18 years of age by election day, not a convicted felon, and not declared mentally incompetent by a court of law.

ATTENTION COLLEGE STUDENTS
Students have two options for which elections they want to participate in, and how they would like to vote.
Students may wish to claim your school/dorm as their residence address. If doing so the student must REGISTER in the COUNTY in which they will be residing. They may then participate in elections under that political jurisdiction.
Register at Home: If your home address is in the state of Texas, you may download the same form located above in the Voter Registration portion, or use the links to request a form be mailed to you.
