County Commissioners Court
▲
Cochran County Commissioners will meet in regular session the 2nd business Monday and next-to-last business day of each month.
Meetings are held at 8:30am in the Commissioners Courtroom on the 1st floor of the Courthouse. Agendas are posted on the Agendas tab at least 5 days prior. Our citizens are always encouraged to attend.
