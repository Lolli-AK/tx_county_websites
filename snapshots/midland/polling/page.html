Daily Voter List

We are required to report to the Secretary of State’s office, by midnight of each day of early voting and on Election Day, with a list of voters who participated on that day. View the list of voters who voted or mailed in an absentee ballot. The list is updated by the Secretary of State’s office daily at 11:00 A.M.  The list is available for download .
