Mission Statement
Our mission is to serve the public by protecting the fundamental right to vote, conducting fair and accessible elections, and providing accurate information and support to all voters. We are committed to maintaining accurate voter registration records, upholding election laws, and fostering public trust through integrity, efficiency, and excellence in service.
