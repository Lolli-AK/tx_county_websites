Quick Access to Online Services

Mitchell County, Texas is characterized by its open plains, rich history, and strong sense of community. Anchored by Colorado City, the county has long been shaped by agriculture, ranching, and steady economic activity that supports the region. With a heritage rooted in hard work and a future focused on opportunity, Mitchell County reflects the balance of tradition and forward momentum that defines West Texas.
