Anderson County, Texas, is a vibrant East Texas community known for its rich history, natural beauty, and strong sense of tradition.
Anchored by the city of Palestine, the county features rolling piney woods, scenic lakes, and a legacy shaped by railroads, agriculture, and local industry. Today, Anderson County offers a welcoming environment with a growing economy, diverse recreational opportunities, and a deep appreciation for its heritage. With a blend of small-town charm and forward-looking progress, it’s a place residents are proud to call home and visitors are glad to discover.
