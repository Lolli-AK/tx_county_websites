NOTICE:
ILLEGAL DUMPING IS AGAINST THE LAW
Archer County wishes to remind everyone that illegal dumping is the law in the State of Texas. Disposing of letter or other solid waste within 300 feet of a public highway, on a right-of-way, on other public or private property, or into inland or coastal water of the state is a violation of Section 365.012 of the Texas Health and Safety Code, and may result in a fine ranging from $500.00 for a Class C Misdemeanor Violation to $4,000.00 and up to a year in jail for Class A Misdemeanor. Please do your part to take care of our county.
