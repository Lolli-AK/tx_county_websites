Welcome to Denton County
The Judge and Commissioners look forward to working with you to make your investment in our area a positive experience. We are committed to the promotion of high-quality development in all parts of the county and to an ongoing improvement in the quality of life for our citizens.
For questions regarding residential property or permitting issues please contact
