Liberty County, Texas
is shaped by a remarkable landscape where the forests and wetlands of the Big Thicket give way to the open Coastal Prairie. The Trinity River has long influenced life across the county, supporting generations of agriculture, commerce, industry, and recreation. With a history reaching back to some of Texas’ earliest settlements and communities that continue to grow and evolve, Liberty County offers a distinctive blend of natural beauty, historic significance, and Southeast Texas character.
