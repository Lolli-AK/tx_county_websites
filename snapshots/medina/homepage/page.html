Established in 1848, Medina County, Texas is located west of San Antonio and remains a historic and growing community dedicated to serving its residents.

Quick Access to Online Services

Medina County Courthouse

Medina County, Texas, blends small-town charm with convenient access to the San Antonio region, making it an ideal place for families and businesses alike. Known for its strong agricultural roots, friendly communities, and beautiful Hill Country landscapes, the county offers a high quality of life with affordable living and room to grow. With a proud heritage and a forward-looking spirit, Medina County continues to be a welcoming place to live, work, and invest.
