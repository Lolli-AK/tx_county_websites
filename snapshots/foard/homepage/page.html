Foard County, Texas, is a North Texas community with deep agricultural roots, quiet surroundings, and strong local character. Anchored by the city of Crowell, the county features wide-open landscapes, long-standing traditions, and a slower pace of life. Foard County remains dedicated to serving its residents through essential services that support public safety, infrastructure, and everyday needs.
