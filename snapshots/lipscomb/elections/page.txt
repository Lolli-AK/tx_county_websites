Please read all the instructions to make sure you will qualify for a Ballot by Mail.
To be eligible to vote early by mail in Texas, you must:
be 65 years of age or older;
be sick or disabled;
be expected to give birth within three weeks before or after Election Day
be out of the county on election day and during the period for early voting by personal appearance; or
be confined in jail or involuntary civil commitment (may only apply for a ballot for one election & any resulting runoff
Once you have sent your ballot by mail to your county's Early Voting Clerk, you can check the status of your mail-in ballot through Ballot by Mail Tracker »
