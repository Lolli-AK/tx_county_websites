Speeding Ticket - Justice of the Peace

Governor Greg Abbott has added Coke County to the list of counties that have been officially designated area for storms over the 4th of July.  As a result, anyone with storm damage to their homes, fences, roadways, hay bales, or other crop damage is eligible to apply for relief from the FEMA Disaster Fund. If you think you have flood or other damage, contact Jeff York at the San Angelo Council of Governments at(325) 947-8729, Ext. 402, or cell (325) 234-0251.  You will need to take pictures of the damage in order to support your claim.
