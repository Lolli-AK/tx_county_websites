Coke County, Texas
is known for its rugged West Texas landscapes, rich ranching heritage, and enduring connection to the Colorado River. Anchored by Robert Lee and Bronte, the county has been shaped by generations of cattle and sheep ranching alongside a long history of oil production. From the waters of E.V. Spence Reservoir to the rolling hills and open country that surround its communities, Coke County offers a distinctive blend of outdoor recreation, agricultural tradition, and the independent character of West Texas.
