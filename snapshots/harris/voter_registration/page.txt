How to Register to Vote
Instructions:
Click on each question below to view more information.
+
Am I Eligible to Vote?
To be eligible to vote in Harris County, Texas, a person must:
be at least 17 years and 10 months to register, and be 18 years old by Election Day;
be a United States citizen; and
be a resident of Harris County.
There are some reasons why a person who meets all these requirements may still NOT be eligible to vote, including if they:
have been declared totally or partially mentally incapacitated by a court and denied the right to vote; or
have been convicted of a felony and are still serving their sentence, including probation and parole.
Please note:
College students have the option to register to vote in the county where they live while they are at school OR at their hometown address.

If you are already registered, it’s still a good idea to double-check your information before the voter registration deadline. Voters may need to update their registration for reasons such as moving to a new address or changing their name.
+
How Do I Register to Vote?
If you are registering to vote in Texas for the first time, you must:
1) Get a voter registration application, fill it out, and sign it.
2) Submit your completed application to the Harris County Tax Office.
See below for instructions on how to get and submit your voter registration application.
Please note:
Texas does not have universal online voter registration. But, if you are already registered to vote in Texas and just need to update information like your name or address, you can make the changes online at
TEXAS.GOV
.
