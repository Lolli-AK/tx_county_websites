Lamb County, Texas is known for its fertile High Plains landscape and the agricultural traditions that have sustained the region for generations. Centered around Littlefield, the county has earned a reputation as a leader in cotton production while continuing to support a diverse farming and ranching economy. With wide-open horizons, hardworking communities, and a deep connection to the land, Lamb County offers a distinctive blend of heritage, innovation, and South Plains pride.
