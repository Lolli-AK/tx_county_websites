Application for a Ballot by Mail

To be eligible to vote early by mail in Texas one of the following must be applicable:

  • Be 65 years or older;
  • Disability
  • Expected absence from the county during both election day and the early voting time period

Confinement in Jail

There are applications online or you may request one by contacting the Elections Administrator.  Applications my be mailed or emailed to the Elections Administrator.
