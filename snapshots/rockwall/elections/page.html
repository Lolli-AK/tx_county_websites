The mission of the Rockwall County Elections Department is to continue to maintain a high level of public confidence in the electoral process. We can only achieve this by maintaining the highest level of transparency. In addition to conducting voter registration and elections with the highest level of professional election standards, integrity, and accuracy. Competence is our watchword and we have always been committed to serving the citizens Rockwall County. My office is always available to assist in any matters election related and myself, as the Appointed Official in Rockwall County, charged with planning, resourcing and executing all Federal, State and Local Elections. I am available anytime business or cell. Everyone in the Elections Department realizes how fortunate we are to live and work in Rockwall County.

Christopher J. Lynch, Elections Administrator
