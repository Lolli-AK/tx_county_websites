Voter Registration
Might be registered already? Check
here
.
To vote in Victoria County, you must be registered at least 30 days
before
the election date at
your Victoria County address.
You are eligible to register to vote if:
You are a United States citizens;
You are a resident Victoria County;
You are at least 17 year and 10 months old, and you are 18 years of age on Election Day;
You are not a convicted felon (you may be eligible to vote if you have completed your sentence, probation, and parole);
You have not been declared by a court exercising probate jurisdiction to be either totally mentally incapacitated or partially mentally incapacitated without the right to vote.
