Voter Registration
Already registered? Check your voter registration status through the
Texas Voter Portal
.
To vote in Victoria County, you must be registered at your Victoria County address at least 30 days before the election date.
You are eligible to register to vote if:
You are a United States citizen.
You are a resident of Victoria County.
You are at least 17 years and 10 months old, and you are 18 years of age on Election Day.
You are not a convicted felon, or you have completed your sentence, probation, and parole.
You have not been declared by a court exercising probate jurisdiction to be either totally mentally incapacitated or partially mentally incapacitated without the right to vote.
Find more information about becoming a registered voter on the
VoteTexas Register to Vote
page.
