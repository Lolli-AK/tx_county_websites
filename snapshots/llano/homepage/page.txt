Llano County, located in the heart of the Texas Hill Country, is known for its scenic waterways, rugged granite outcrops, and ranching traditions.
Centered around the City of Llano, the county is shaped by the Llano River and a strong appreciation for the outdoors. Agriculture, tourism, and small businesses play an important role in the local economy, supported by a community that values stewardship and a relaxed lifestyle. Llano County offers a balance of natural beauty and local character for residents and visitors alike.
