November 9, 2026 Meeting Agenda
IMPORTANT UPDATE:
Beginning October 2026, the Dallam County Appraisal District (CAD)
will manage tax collections for Dallam County.
Please note that your 2026 Tax Statement now includes Dallam County taxes alongside
the other ad valorem taxes previously paid to the CAD.
You can now pay all of these taxes together in one of three ways:
1. In Person: 401 Denver Ave., Dalhart, TX 79022
2. By Mail: Dallam CAD, PO Box 579, Dalhart, TX 79022
3. Online: www.dallamcad.org
For assistance, please call 806-249-6767.
