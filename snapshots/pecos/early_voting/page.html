Registering to vote for the first time:

To register to vote in Texas, simply complete a voter registration application and return it to your county election office at least 30 days before the upcoming election date.

  • Complete an application using the SOS ONLINE VOTER REGISTRATION APPLICATION .
    Fill in the required information, print, sign and mail the completed application directly to your county election office. Direct links to the application:
    • Texas Voter Registration Application
    • Solicitud de registro electoral en Texas
  • Contact or visit the county election office to complete the voter registration process:
