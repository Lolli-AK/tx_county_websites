November 3, 2026, General and Special Elections
Early Vote Centers will be open from
Monday, October 19 – Friday, October 30
(Mon-Sat: 7:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m.; Sun: 12:00 p.m. - 7:00 p.m.)
The deadline to apply for a mail ballot is October 23.
Click here
for the application. Please fill it out, print it, and mail it to our office before the deadline.
Vote Centers will accept voters from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on
Tuesday, November 3, Election Day.
Visit our “What’s on my Ballot?” page and enter your name or address to see all the contests and candidates you are eligible to vote on! (You can bring handwritten notes or printed sample ballots to the voting booth; just be sure to take it with you when you leave.)
