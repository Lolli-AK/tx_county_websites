    • Elections

    It is the mission of the Tarrant County Elections Administration to register eligible voters and to provide free, fair, accurate, and secure elections in a transparent manner consistent with all applicable laws.


    One of the most important functions of county government is to oversee the electoral process. The Elections Department has the responsibility of conducting all federal, state and county elections and certain elections for municipalities, independent school districts and other political subdivisions that contract with Tarrant County for election services.

    Tarrant County Elections Launches Voter Precinct Change Lookup Online Tool

    Tarrant County Elections has launched Voter Precinct Change Look Up, a convenient online tool that allows registered voters to enter their address and view both their current voting districts and their updated voter precinct information effective January 1, 2026.

    We now have a new Ballot Verification system.  The Ballot Verifier allows members of the public access to every single cast ballot image and Cast Vote Record (CVR) in every Tarrant County election, starting with the complete March 2024 primary election - with additional elections coming soon.
