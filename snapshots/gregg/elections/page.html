Additional Voting Information

– To vote, you must be registered at least 30 days before Election Day .
– Bring an approved photo ID to vote. If you do not have one, you may complete a Reasonable Impediment Declaration and provide an acceptable supporting ID .
– We encourage you to vote early . Waiting until Election Day may result in longer lines .
– Use the tabs above for information on registration, the ballot, early voting, voting by mail, and Election Day voting .

Curbside Voting
Curbside Voting is available to voters who are physically unable to enter the polling place without assistance or who may risk injury by doing so.

To use curbside voting, please park in the designated curbside space and call or text the number posted on the sign. You may also contact the entity conducting the election. Inform the election worker of your need to vote curbside and provide a description of your vehicle. An election worker will bring the voting equipment to you.

Note: Rain or other inclement weather may delay curbside voting since the equipment cannot get wet.
