Register to Vote
Voter Registration
By law, you must be registered to vote at least 30 days prior to Election Day.
Please register now – do not wait until the last minute!
How to Register to Vote
Call our office at
903-236-8458
. We will mail you an application. You must receive the completed form by the deadline.
Come by our office and fill out an application in person.
Print the online
Voter Registration Form
(Opens PDF)
. This is
not
an online registration.
You
must print
the form,
sign
it, and
mail it
or
hand deliver
it to the election office.
You may also visit the state’s website:
VoteTexas.Gov
Office Location and Contact Information
Our office is on the first floor of the Gregg County Parking Facililty across the street from the Courthouse.
Physical Address:
100 E Methvin St, Suite 130
Longview, TX 75601
Our mailing address is:
P O Box 2827,
Longview, TX 75606
After Registration
After you register to vote, you should receive your voter certificate. Keep this card! It verifies that you are registered to vote and shows your voting precinct and districts. If you need a replacement voter certificate, call our office at
903-236-8458
.
