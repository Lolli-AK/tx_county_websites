MILITARY & OVERSEAS VOTERS - FPCAS
Visit
www.fvap.gov
for comprehensive information for military and overseas voters. FVAP also offers a wizard to help you complete your FPCA electronically (Note: Once completed, the FPCA must be printed and signed before it is submitted to our office)
Or download a Federal Post Card Application (FPCA)
here
.
Who is eligible to use the FPCA?
Members of the U.S. armed forces and merchant marines, their dependents who are stationed outside of Williamson County, and U.S. citizens living abroad are eligible. (Federal voting law defines "overseas" as anywhere outside the United States, including Canada and Mexico.)
Voters who indicate they are U.S. citizens residing overseas and do not intend to return are only eligible to vote on federal races.
The FPCA
MUST
contain an address where the applicant is located outside of Williamson County. Voters who are currently residing in Williamson County are not eligible to use the FPCA.
What are the benefits of the FPCA?
Voters who submit an FPCA will automatically receive ballots for all elections in which they are eligible to vote for a period of one calendar year.
FPCA voters who are returning their ballots from outside of the United States must have their ballots post marked by 7:00PM CST on Election Day but have an additional five days after the date of the election for our office to receive the marked ballot.
FPCA voters can track the status of their ballots
here
.
FPCA voters who have a valid FPCA on file but have not received their ballot may also be eligible to vote using a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB). Please visit
www.fvap.gov
for more information.
