Mission Statement
"The mission of the Guadalupe County Elections Office is to ensure a fair, transparent, and efficient electoral process that upholds the democratic rights of all eligible voters. We are committed to providing accurate and timely information, ensuring accessibility for all voters, and maintaining the integrity of the voting process. Our goal is to foster public trust in elections by administering them with professionalism, impartiality, and accountability."
Location: 3251 State Highway 123 North in Seguin, just north of IH 10, Seguin, TX 78155.

VOLUNTEER DEPUTY REGISTRAR REQUIREMENTS
If you are interested in becoming a Volunteer Deputy Voter Registrar, you must attend training. Classes will be held on the 2nd Wednesday of each month beginning at 3 p.m. at the Seguin Elections Office. Please call 830-303-6363 to register to attend.
