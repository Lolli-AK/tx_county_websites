AM I REGISTERED TO VOTE
?
Postage-paid voter registration applications (for new registrations or updates) are available at the Taylor County Elections Office or click the link below to complete and print a voter registration application. Completed applications must be returned or postmarked by the 30th day before any election in which you desire to vote.
