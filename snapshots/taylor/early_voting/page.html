  1. Early Voting Rosters

Section 87.121(g) Texas Election Code - The list for persons who vote an early voting ballot by personal appearance shall be made available to the public no later than 11 a.m. on the day after the ballot is cast.

Section 87.121(h) Texas Election Code - The list for person who vote an early voting ballot by mail shall be made available to the public no later than 11 a.m. on the day after the early voting clerk receives the mail ballot.
