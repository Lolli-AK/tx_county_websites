The list for persons who vote an early voting ballot by personal appearance shall be made available to the public no later than 11 a.m. on the day

The list for person who vote an early voting ballot by mail shall be made available to the public no later than 11 a.m. on the day
after
the early voting clerk receives the mail ballot.
