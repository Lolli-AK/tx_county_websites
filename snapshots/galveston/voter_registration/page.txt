Voter Registration
The County Clerk serves as the Voter Registrar for Galveston County. The office is located at the Galveston County Records and Election Center in Texas City.
Questions about eligibility, registration or updating your registration can be answered by clicking the link below or contacting our office at 409-766-2280. (

Volunteer Deputy Registrar
To become a Volunteer Deputy Registrar for Galveston County please complete the following:
Fill out an application
Complete the presentation from the Secretary of State
Study the practice exam
Call and schedule an appointment to take the exam in our Texas City office 409-766-2280
Once you have completed the exam with an 80% or more, we will provide you an Official Certificate and all of your documentation.
If you are currently a VDR in another county and would like to become a Galveston County VDR please send our office a copy of your certificate along with your application.  Our office will prepare all of your documentation as well as any needed supplies for pick up.
