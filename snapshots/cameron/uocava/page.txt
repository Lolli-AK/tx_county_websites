Federal Post Card Application (FPCA) and the Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB)
Updates have been made to the Federal Post Card Application (FPCA) and the Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB).
These updates were made to eliminate voter confusion about the information they are required to provide under Federal law and their eligibility to use these forms to register and request an absentee ballot.
If the voter already has a previous version of the FPCA or FWAB on file with their election office, they do not need to submit a new form.
States may process previous versions of the FPCA and FWAB forms submitted before their statutory deadlines for receipt.
