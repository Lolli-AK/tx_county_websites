Prior Election Results

The Ellis County Elections Administrator will publish unofficial election results for all elections conducted by this office. Election results are unofficial until canvassed or adopted by the political subdivision holding the election (state, party, county, city or school). Official results are kept by the filing officer (city, school, county or state). Unofficial election results are produced directly from our tabulation system. Results posted on this page are unofficial.
