Our New Mass Notification System

Public Notice
Jim Wells County
Community Development Block Grant - Disaster Recovery
Jim Wells County invites all citizens to review and comment on a proposed amendment to its 2018 CDBG-MIT Program Contract No. 22-085-027-D268. This amendment will modify the project budget by decreasing the amount of funds allocated to construction and increasing the amount of funds allocated to acquisition.  The amendment will allow the county to complete the acquisition of property needed for the scope of the project.
A copy of the proposed budget modification amendment is available for review during normal business hours at the County Judge's Office. Written comments may be submitted to Isabel Trevino, Jim Wells County, at 200 N. Almond St, Alice TX 78332 for at least 15 days starting on 9/3/2026 and ending 9/17/2026.
Aviso Público
Jim Wells County
Subvención para el Desarrollo Comunitario - Recuperación ante Desastres
El condado de Jim Wells invita a todos los ciudadanos a revisar y comentar una propuesta de enmienda al Contrato No. 22-085-027-D268 de su Programa CDBG-MIT de 2018. Esta enmienda modificará el presupuesto del proyecto reduciendo los fondos asignados a la construcción y aumentando los destinados a la adquisición. La enmienda permitirá al condado completar la adquisición de la propiedad necesaria para el alcance del proyecto.
Se puede consultar una copia de la propuesta de enmienda de modificación presupuestaria en la Oficina del Juez del Condado (County Judge’s Office) durante el horario laboral habitual. Los comentarios por escrito pueden enviarse a Isabel Travino, del condado de Jim Wells, a la dirección 200 N Almond St, Alice, TX 78332, durante un periodo de al menos 15 días, comenzando el 3 de septiembre de 2026 y finalizando el 17 de septiembre de 2026.
Public Service Announcement
Please be informed that the Trains is passing through Jim Wells County, Texas, faster following railroad track upgrades.  Follow recently completed track infrastructure upgrades in the region, the maximum track speed for freight trains operating through Jim Wells County, Texas will rise this week.  Effective on April 24, trains will pass through the area at a maximum of 60 mph, up from the current maximum of 50-55 mph.

State of Texas Emergency
Assistance Registry (STEAR)
The State of Texas offers the option to register with the STEAR program, a free registry that provides local emergency management planners and responders with information related to your needs during an emergency.
Who Should Register?
People with disabilities
People who are medically fragile
People with functional needs such as:
Limited Mobility
Communication barriers
Require additional medical assistance during an emergency event
Require personal care assistance
People who require transportation assistance
Register online at
stear.tdem.texas.gov
.
Call 2-1-1 or use your video phone relay option of choice.
Registering in STEAR
DOES NOT
guarantee you will receive a specific service during emergencies.
