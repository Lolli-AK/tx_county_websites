Accessible Voting: What if I have a permanent disability and want to vote in person?

It is your right to vote privately and independently. All our polling locations offer CURBSIDE VOTING, so look for the spot designated with the sign, park your car in the designated spot, call the number, and one of our Election Workers will be with you shortly. We also offer wheelchair-accessible voting machines inside our polling location, as well as features to help those who are hard of hearing or cannot use their fingers to fill in or tap the screens on our machines.
