Welcome to Young County, Texas


Young County is in north central Texas. Graham, the county seat of government,
is in the southeastern section of the county.

The county was named for Col. William C. Young.

Notice About 2026 Tax Rate

Young County Commissioners Court will hold a PUBLIC HEARING on the proposed FY2026/27 budget. The public hearing will be held in the Young County Courthouse, 516 Fourth Street, in the Young County Court Room #106, at 9:30 a.m. Monday, August 24, 2026. Any person may attend and may participate in the hearing. At the conclusion of the hearing the Court may take action on the Proposed Budget which may include adoption, changing or postponing action for not more than seven (7) days. The Budget Officers Proposed Budget shall be filed with the Office of Young County Clerk, Room 104, in the Young County Courthouse for public inspection not later than 4:00 p.m., Friday, August 14, 2026.

THIS BUDGET WILL RAISE LESS REVENUE FROM PROPERTY TAXES THAN LAST YEAR’S BUDGET BY AN AMOUNT OF $76,467, WHICH IS A 0.9% DECREASE FROM LAST YEAR’S BUDGET. THE PROPERTY TAX REVENUE TO BE RAISED FROM NEW PROPERTY ADDED TO THE TAX ROLL THIS YEAR IS $254,008.
