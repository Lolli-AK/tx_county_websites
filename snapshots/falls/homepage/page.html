Falls County, Texas, is a Central Texas community with a strong agricultural foundation and a rich historical heritage. Centered around the county seat of Marlin, the county is home to rural landscapes, the Brazos River, and a network of small communities that contribute to its local character. Falls County is committed to providing essential services that support public safety, infrastructure, and the well-being of its residents, while preserving the traditions that define the area.
