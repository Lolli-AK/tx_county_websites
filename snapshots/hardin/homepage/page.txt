Information, Links, and Resources
Quick Access to Online Services
Hardin County, Texas is located in Southeast Texas and is known for its wooded landscapes, riverways, and access to outdoor recreation.
Home to communities such as Kountze, Lumberton, Rose Hill Acres, Sour Lake and Silsbee, the county offers a mix of small-town living and nearby regional amenities. The local economy is supported by forestry, energy, manufacturing, and agriculture, while rivers, public lands, and proximity to the Big Thicket provide opportunities to enjoy the outdoors. Hardin County brings together natural surroundings, established communities, and room to grow.
