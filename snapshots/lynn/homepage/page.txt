Where South Plains Roots Run Deep.
Lynn County, Texas is known for its rich South Plains heritage, from the early ranches and frontier history surrounding Tahoka Lake and Double Lakes to the cotton fields that have shaped its economy for generations. Anchored by Tahoka and communities including O'Donnell, Wilson, and New Home, the county reflects a history built on ranching, agriculture, and the determination of those who settled the High Plains. Today, its expansive landscapes and enduring connection to the land offer a distinctive glimpse into the history and character of rural West Texas.
