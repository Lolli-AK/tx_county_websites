County Profile (click here)
Clay County covers an area of 1,088.7 square miles of
land which is 696,320 acres, and 28 square miles of water.
The population is 10,218, according to the 2020 census.
Schools in Clay county with their estimated
enrollments include
Bellevue ISD, with 234 students,
Henrietta ISD with 886 students,
Midway ISD, with 137 students, and
Petrolia ISD with 510.
Clay County Memorial Hospital has 25 beds.
Major roadways in the county are
US HWY 82,
US HWY 287 N,
US HWY 287 S,
ST HWY 148 S, ST HWY 148 N, and
ST HWY 79 N.
The county seat for Clay County is Henrietta.
