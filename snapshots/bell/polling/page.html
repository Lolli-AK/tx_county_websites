Notice of Voting Priority
IAW Sec. 63.0015 of the Texas Elections Code, ACCEPTING VOTERS WITH CERTAIN DISABILITIES.
New law : An election officer may give voting priority to individuals with a mobility problem that substantially impairs the person's ability to move around.
Ley nueva : Un oficial electoral puede dar prioridad a la orden de votaci ón personas con un problema de movilidad que sustancialmente afecta la capacidad de la persona para moverse.
