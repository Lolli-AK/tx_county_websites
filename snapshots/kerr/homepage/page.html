Kerr County Courthouse CLOSED

Courthouse Offices Relocated to Temporary Sites

The Kerr County Courthouse is CLOSED to the public.

Due to severe flood damage on the lower level of the Kerr County Courthouse, all offices housed in the courthouse have been relocated to other county facilities.

OPEN TO THE PUBLIC:

Need to know where to find us? Here are the following 3 major public-facing offices providing public services:

  • KERR COUNTY TAX OFFICE - The Kerr County Tax Assessor-Collector's Office is now open to the public in the West Kerr County Courthouse Annex, 510 College Street in Ingram. It can be reached by calling 830-792-2241. Hours are: 8 a.m.-4:30 p.m., Mondays through Fridays

  • KERR COUNTY CLERK'S OFFICE - Those who need services provided by the Kerr County Clerk can find that office in the the Hill Country Youth Event Center, 3785 TX27 in Kerrville, by entering on the far right-side of the building. It can be reached by phone at 830-792-2255. Hours are 8 a.m.-4:30 p.m., Mondays through Fridays.

  • KERR COUNTY DISTRICT CLERK'S OFFICE - Citizens needing services in the Kerr County District Clerk's Office can find it in its temporary home at the Hill Country Youth Event Center, 3785 TX27 in Kerrville, by entering the far left-side of the building where the "Office" sign is located. It can be reachedby phone at 830-792-2281. Hours are 7:30 a.m.-4 p.m., Mondays through Fridays.

OTHER COUNTY OFFICES:

At this time, all other county offices are inaccessible to the public in temporary quarters that are restricted to employee-only entry.

Debris Removal

The county is actively working to implement a safe, effective debris removal plan. Details to come. Our top priority is the safety and well-being of our citizens. Following last year's flood, debris removal was conducted in record-breaking time, but even then it took 3 weeks to get going, said Kerr County Commissioner (Pct. 4) Don Harris. "We appreciate everyone's patience on this."

Residents can act now to sort their debris and put at the road's edge (not in the roadway) in separate stacks according to material -- vegetative, electronics, large appliances, etc. NO DUMPING will be allowed, according to Kerr County Sheriff Larry Leitha. When they are determined, further details of the debris removal plan will e announced. Stay tuned!

Report Damage from Flood

'No Matter How Small'

Kerr County Judge Rob Kelly and Commissioners urge residents to report any level of flood damaged sustained to your home, business or agricultural property.

Do so by using the TDEM’s Individual State of Texas Assistance Tool (iSTAT)portal, found at damage.tdem.texas.gov or by using your phone to scan this QR code:

This helps inform officials of the extent of damages, aids in connecting Texans with available services and helps determine the state’s eligibility for various forms of federal disaster assistance.

Please only submit one survey per affected address.

A minimum of 1 photo is required with the submission, but users can enter up to 10 photos per submission.

As a reminder, reporting damages is a voluntary activity, is not a substitute for reporting damage to your insurance agency, and does not guarantee disaster relief assistance. If you have insurance, contact your insurance provider.

New World Screwworm

What You Need to Know

New World Screwworm (NWS) is a parasitic fly that poses a serious threat to livestock, exotic livestock, fowl and exotic fowl in Texas.

NWS flies lay eggs in wounds or soft tissues of live warm-blooded animals; the larvae hatch and burrow into living tissue, leading to the painful condition NWS myiasis. As the infestation worsens and the wounds become larger, it can cause serious injury, secondary infection and possibly death if not treated.

Due to the detection of New World Screwworm in neighboring Gillespie County, some areas of Kerr, as well as Kimble County, have been grouped with part of Gillespie County into Infested Zone #03.

To protect the health of animals in this state, reduce the spread of NWS to non-infested animals, and prevent NWS from establishing in new areas, a quarantine has been imposed by the Texas Animal Health Commission and animal movements are restricted.

Please see the full details, as detailed by the Texas Animal Health Commission, in the official order in the downloadable/viewable document below:

Order Declaring Animal Movement Restrictions (PDF)

SCREWWORM FACTS:

Did you know that screwworms do NOT pose a food safety issue? The USDA states the parasite cannot spread through the consumption of meat, poultry or dairy product.

Screwworms are not a contagious disease or virus. Transmission only occurs when female screwworm flies lay eggs directly in an open wound or exposed mucous membrane of a warm-blooded animal.

For more FACTS and detailed information, visit:

Screwworm.gov
