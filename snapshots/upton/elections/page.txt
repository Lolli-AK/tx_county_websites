Become a Pollworker

Become a Poll Worker
You must be a registered voter of Upton County to be a poll worker.
Student poll workers do not have to be a registered voter but they must be between 16-18 years of age and a US citizen.
Poll Worker Training
All poll workers are required to attend a training session before each election, our office will provide trainings. Trainings are taking place currently.
Poll Worker Pay
The 2024 pay rates for poll workers are:
Election Judges: $20.00/hour
Election Clerk: $18.00/hour
If you are interested in becoming a poll worker, please email Candise at
cdfox@co.upton.tx.us
Conviértase en trabajador electoral
Debe ser un votante registrado del condado de Upton para ser trabajador electoral.
Los estudiantes trabajadores electorales no tienen que ser votantes registrados, pero deben tener entre 16 y 18 años y ser ciudadanos estadounidenses.
Capacitación de trabajadores electorales
Todos los trabajadores electorales deben asistir a una sesión de capacitación antes de cada elección; nuestra oficina brindará capacitación. Actualmente se están realizando capacitaciones.
Pago de los trabajadores electorales
Las tasas salariales de 2024 para los trabajadores electorales son:
Jueces electorales: $16.00/hora
Secretario Electoral: $14.00/hora
Si está interesado en convertirse en trabajador electoral, envíe un correo electrónico a Candise a cdfox@co.upton.tx.us.

Upton County Voter Registration Card Mail-Out Update
The Upton County Elections Office has received questions regarding the delay in mailing voter registration cards. We want to provide clear information on why this is occurring and what it means for our voters.
Upton County, along with many counties across Texas, has experienced delays due to the implementation of the state’s new Texas Election Administration Management (TEAM) system, which is used to process voter registrations and maintain the official list of registered voters. Since the rollout of this system, counties have faced temporary interruptions in the ability to print and mail voter registration cards.
Although the physical cards have been delayed, voter registration records remain active and accurate.
Important information for Upton County voters:
If you submitted a voter registration application or update, you are still officially registered.
No action is required if your name and address have not changed.
Voters must present a valid photo ID when voting.
Registration status can be verified at VoteTexas.gov or by contacting the Upton County Elections Office.
Voter registration cards include helpful information such as your voter ID number, precinct, and districts; however, not having the physical card will not prevent you from voting in upcoming elections.
The Upton County Elections Office continues to work closely with the Texas Secretary of State’s Office and will mail voter registration cards as soon as the issue is resolved. Updates will be shared as additional information becomes available.
Thank you for your patience and understanding as we work through this statewide issue. Our priority is ensuring every eligible voter in Upton County has the information they need to participate in the election process.

Am I qualified to vote by mail?
Voters who wish to vote by mail must meet at least one of the four eligibility requirements listed below:
1.
65 years of age or older
2.
Sick or disabled
3.
Expected to give birth within 3 weeks before or after Election Day
4.
Expected Absence from the County* (Must be absent during the entire early voting period, Election Day, AND provide an out-of-county address where your ballot can be mailed, AND include dates you can receive mail at the out-of-county address.)
5.
Confined in jail or Involuntary Civil Commitment, but otherwise eligible to vote

Who is eligible to use the FPCA?
Members of the U.S. armed forces and merchant marines, their dependents who are stationed outside of Upton County, and U.S. citizens living abroad are eligible. (Federal voting law defines "overseas" as anywhere outside the United States, including Canada and Mexico.)
Voters who indicate they are U.S. citizens residing overseas and do not intend to return are only eligible to vote on federal races.
The FPCA MUST contain an address where the applicant is located outside of Upton County. Voters who are currently residing in Upton County are not eligible to use the FPCA.

Accessibility Statement for Upton County, Texas
▲
We want everyone who visits the Upton County elections website to feel welcome and find the experience rewarding, going above and beyond the requirements stated in Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).
What are we doing?
To help us make the Upton County elections website a positive place for everyone, we've been referring to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities, more compatible with assistive technology, and more user friendly for everyone.
How are we doing?
We've worked hard on the Upton County elections website, and we've hired the certified professionals to help us achieve our goal of WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility.
The following pages/documents included in the scope of work are:
Elections
Global elements
The accessibility experts we’ve hired will conduct manual testing on a Windows or Mac computer using NVDA,JAWs, or VoiceOver, as appropriate. Proprietary software is used for all automated testing.
Upton County Elections is dedicated to ensuring our website remains accessible to the widest possible audience. We conduct regular monitoring and maintenance to uphold our commitment to accessibility. We welcome feedback from all visitors regarding any accessibility barriers encountered on our election website or online content.
We have a Disability Complaint form.pdf that you can fill out and email the ADA Coordinator directly or can mail it to the address listed below.
We will make every reasonable effort to accommodate all requests and will respond to any comments or concerns within fourteen (14) calendar days of receipt.
For more information, please review our Upton County Elections ADA Grievance Procedure.pdf

Curbside Voting:
Curbside voting is designed for voters who are physically unable to enter the poll place. It allows eligible individuals to vote from their vehicle. Voters can request curbside voting at any early voting site or election day polling place. Park in the designated space, call the number on the sign and a poll worker will assist you.
Some documents may not be fully accessible to all users, such as forms filled out by hand and digitized via scanning. While we have applied OCR techniques to make them as usable as possible, there are cases where they do not perform well in screen readers. If you need access to this information, please call us at (432) 693-2014 and we would be happy to provide the information in an alternate format.
