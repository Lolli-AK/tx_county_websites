Application to Vote By Mail -
Solicitud para recibir una boleta por correo
▲
Voting by mail allows eligible Texas voters to receive and return their ballot without visiting a polling place in person. This option supports voters who are unable to appear in person due to age, health, scheduled absence, or other qualifying circumstances.
Eligibility Requirements
Under Texas law, you may qualify to vote by mail if you meet one of the following criteria:
•
You will be 65 years of age or older on Election Day
•
You have a sickness or physical disability
•
You are expected to give birth within three weeks before or after Election Day
•
You will be outside of your county for the entire early voting period and on Election Day
•
You are confined in jail but are otherwise legally eligible to vote
How to Apply
1?. Complete an Application for Ballot by Mail (ABBM)
ABBM Application
2?. Provide required identification (Texas Driver License or State ID number, or the last four digits of your Social Security Number)
3?. Submit the signed application by mail or in-person to the County Early Voting Clerk
Once approved, a ballot will be mailed to you. Follow all instructions carefully and return your ballot by the statutory deadline to ensure it is counted.
For additional guidance or to request an ABBM, please contact the Elections Administration office.
________________________________________
Votar por Correo en Texas
Votar por correo permite que los votantes elegibles en Texas reciban y devuelvan su boleta sin tener que presentarse físicamente en un centro de votación. Esta opción está disponible para quienes no pueden votar en persona debido a edad, salud, ausencia programada u otras circunstancias permitidas por ley.
Requisitos de Elegibilidad
Según la ley estatal de Texas, usted puede calificar para votar por correo si cumple una de las siguientes condiciones:
•
Tendrá 65 años o más el Día de la Elección
•
Tiene una enfermedad o discapacidad física
•
Se espera que dé a luz dentro de las tres semanas antes o después del Día de la Elección
•
Estará fuera de su condado durante todo el período de votación anticipada y el Día de la Elección
•
Está confinado en la cárcel, pero aún conserva el derecho legal de votar
Cómo Solicitar Una Boleta
1?. Complete la Solicitud de Boleta por Correo (ABBM)
ABBM Applicacion
2?. Incluya su número de licencia de Texas, identificación estatal, o los últimos cuatro dígitos de su Seguro Social
3?. Envíe la solicitud firmada por correo o en persona a la Oficina del Secretario de Votación Anticipada del Condado
Una vez aprobada, se le enviará la boleta a su domicilio. Siga todas las instrucciones y devuélvala antes de la fecha límite para garantizar que sea contada.
Para obtener orientación adicional o solicitar una ABBM, comuníquese con nuestra oficina.

Fannin County Elections Administration Office - High School & College Student Clerk Program
▲
We are seeking qualified high school & College students to serve as Student Election Clerks for the upcoming 2026 election cycle. This program allows students to participate in the democratic process while earning valuable experience that counts toward community service hours. Through this educational experience, students can also receive professional references that can be used for college, scholarship, and job applications.
Program Overview
• Students serve as election clerks under the supervision of election officials.
• Duties include greeting voters, explaining voting procedures, assisting with voting equipment, and helping set up/close polling sites.
• Students receive paid compensation at the same hourly rate as regular clerks.
Requirements
• 16 years old on Election Day
• Be a U.S. citizen
• Be enrolled in a public, private, or home school program
• Obtain written permission from a parent/guardian (if a minor)
• Complete election worker training
Benefits to Students
• Paid work experience
• Resume and college application builder
• Get community service hours
• Use this experience as a reference for college admission and scholarship applications
• Opportunity to serve the community
