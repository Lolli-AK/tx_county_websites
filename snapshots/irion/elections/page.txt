Ballots
The Ballots page of Irion County, Texas provides voters with access to election ballot information, including sample ballots for upcoming elections and voting-related resources. It helps residents review candidates, offices, and measures that will appear on their ballots before Election Day, ensuring they are informed and prepared to vote. The page serves as a convenient resource for accessing official ballot documents, election notices, and other election-related information published by the Irion County Elections Office.
City & School Elections
The City School Election page provides information about elections related to the local school district, including election dates, candidate filings, voting details, and official notices. Residents can access important election resources to stay informed and participate in decisions affecting their local schools and community.
Notices
The Election Notices page provides official election announcements, polling information, sample ballots, testing notices, election results, and other important voting-related documents for Irion County residents. Voters can access current and archived election notices to stay informed about upcoming elections and election procedures.
Voting By Mail
The Irion County Voting by Mail page explains how eligible voters can apply for a mail ballot, submit applications, and access county election clerk contact information and voting-by-mail resources.
Voter ID Information
The Texas voter ID requirements page explains the acceptable forms of identification needed to vote in person and the alternative options available for voters who cannot reasonably obtain a photo ID.
Voter Registration
The Texas voter registration page provides information on voter eligibility, registration methods, deadlines, and how to update or verify your voter registration status in Texas.
Secretary of State
This website is the official portal of the Texas Secretary of State, providing access to government services and information. It allows users to manage business filings, access election and voting resources, and find various public records and services.
