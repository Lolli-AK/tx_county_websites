Burn Ban is in effect for Navarro County!
Navarro County, Texas
is known for its historic ties to early oil production and the character of Corsicana, where a revitalized downtown and local culture bring new energy to a well-established community. With scenic countryside and a strong agricultural foundation, the county blends heritage with forward momentum, offering a place that feels both rooted and evolving. From time spent on Richland-Chambers Reservoir to everyday life across its communities, Navarro County offers a setting that is both active and connected.
