The Fort Bend County Elections Administrator conducts Federal, State, and County elections. The Election Administrator also conducts elections for other governmental entities that may contract with the County for election services.
Conducting elections includes, but is not limited to
:
Input to and maintenance of
voter registration database
.
Training
and supervision of precinct judges, early voting judges and central counting station personnel.
Administration of early
voting in person
and early
voting by mail
. You may view processing of ballots
here
.
Supervision of balloting.
Supervision of tabulation and preparation of final
canvassing reports
.
The Elections Administrator preserves voter registration applications, official election documents and candidate campaign disclosures.
