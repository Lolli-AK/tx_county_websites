First Time Voters Guide: How to Cast Your Ballot in 7 Steps:
SECURING THE VOTE
Its the top priority of the Marionc County elections office to make sure that our elections are secure.  For more information about our process to ensure the integrity of our elections,
click here
.  (This will send you to our Election Security webpage)
VOTING BY MAIL
Interested in voting by mail this election?  Please visit our
Early Voting By Mail
page on our website for general information about the process as well as a link to the application to vote by mail!
We are here to serve our voters!  If you have any questions or need any help, please let us know!
IMPORTANT DATES
Saturday, January 1, 2024 – First day to apply for ballot by mail
Monday, October 7, 2024 – Last day to Register to Vote
Monday, October 21, 2024 – First day of Early Voting
Friday, October 25, 2024 – Last day to apply for ballot by mail (Received, not postmarked)
Friday, November 1, 2024 – Last day of Early Voting
Tuesday, November 5, 2024 – Election Day
