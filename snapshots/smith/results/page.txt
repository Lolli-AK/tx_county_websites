Current Election Results
Early Voting and Absentee results will be posted here on Election Night at 7 pm.
After all locations deliver their Election Day ballots, those results will be tabulated, and the resulting report(s) will replace the previous document posted.
The final results will be posted after Canvassing (this usually takes about 2 weeks after an election). If you have any questions, please contact our office at 903-590-4777.
