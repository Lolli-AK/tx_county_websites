Current Election Information

A Photo ID is required to vote. Here is a list of the IDs (PDF) you can use.

New : This Interactive Map of Smith County allows a user to enter their address to view the precinct in which they reside, as well as the districts they are eligible to vote on. Users can also view where an address is located in relation to a polling place, either early voting or on Election Day.

November 3, 2026 November General Election

Important Dates

  • January 2, 2026 - First Day to Submit an Application for Ballot by Mail
  • October 5, 2026, Last Day to Register to Vote. You can check your registration status and address .
  • October 19, 2026 - First Day of Early Voting by Personal Appearance
  • October 23, 2026 - Last Day to Submit an Application for a Ballot by Mail (received, not postmarked)
  • October 30, 2026 - Last Day of Early Voting by Personal Appearance
  • November 3, 2026 - Election Day
  • November 4, 2026 - Last Day to Receive Ballot by Mail (postmarked by 7 pm on November 3, 2026)

The Smith County Elections Office will begin live streaming the voted ballots when early voting ballots are delivered to the office in their ballot boxes the evening of October 30, 2026 and will continue to do so 24 hours a day until after the votes are canvassed. This is being done to comply with Section 127.1232(b) of the Texas Election Code.
