Public Information Requests
▲
A Public Information or Open Records request is a request for public information contained in the files or records of a Texas governmental agency. They are governed by the provisions of the Public Information Act (
http://www.statutes.legis.state.tx.us/Docs/GV/pdf/GV.552.pdf
), Texas Government Code, Chapter 552.
Please note the Public Information Act does not require governmental bodies to create new information, perform legal research, or to answer questions. The request must ask for records or information already in existence. Karnes County will respond within ten (10) business days to information requests.
To submit a request, please click on the link below to access the request form.
Karnes County Public Information Request Form
Please submit the completed form to the Karnes County Attorney's office. The mailing address is 101 N. Panna Maria Ave, Suite 302, Karnes City, Texas 78118.
To submit a request via email, please send to
public.records@co.karnes.tx.us
.
If you are requesting an accident report, please click on the link below regarding your request.
