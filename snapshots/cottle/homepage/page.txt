Embracing our proud frontier heritage and rural community spirit, Cottle County welcomes you to a peaceful, authentic way of life in the heart of Northwest Texas.
Information, Links, and Resources
Quick Access to Online Services
Cottle County,
located in the rolling plains of Northwest Texas, was established by the Texas Legislature in 1876 and officially organized in 1892. Named after George Washington Cottle, who died defending the Alamo, its history is deeply tied to massive cattle ranches, early agriculture, and the railroad.
