Election Day Vote Information

On election day, find information on locations for voting. To check if you are registered to vote and to find your election day polling place visit our Voter Registration Check & Polling Location Search .

Election day voting is from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. For more election resources, visit the Elections homepage .
