Welcome!
The Bexar County Elections Department
is responsible for voter registration activities and election operations throughout Bexar County. This includes 806 voting precincts, 53 political subdivisions, 20 Independent School Districts, 29 Cities, 3 Military Installations (including Lackland AFB, Fort Sam Houston, and Randolph Air Force Base), and 1,302,585
registered voters.
Our Mission
To plan and conduct Federal, State, County, and contract elections and to provide voter registration services to the citizens of Bexar County efficiently, accurately, and in accordance with established laws, regulations, and governmental policies.
