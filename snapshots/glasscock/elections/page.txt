Register to Vote
Well before each election, check and make sure you are registered to vote:
Am I Registered to Vote?
If you are registered, but you need to update your name or address:
Update Your Voter Registration Name or Address
You can get your Voter Unique Identifier (VUID) by visiting the "Am I Registered to Vote?" link above.
The County must receive your voter registration application at least 30 days before an election.
Ways to register to vote:
In person.
Visit the County Clerk's office.
Online/Print and Mail.
Fill out your voter registration application online here.
Print, sign, add postage, and mail it to the County Clerk's office.
By mail.
You can pick up a postage-free mail-in application at the Voter Registrar office, the Department of Public Safety, the public library, or high schools.
With a Volunteer Deputy Registrar
(VDR).
A VDR can provide the form to fill out and deliver your completed form to the Voter Registrar office.

Vote by Mail
You may vote early by mail if you are:
65 years of age or older on Election Day;
Sick or disabled;
Expecting to give birth within three weeks before or after Election Day;
Absent from the county of registration during the Early Voting period and on Election Day;
Civilly committed under Chapter 841 of the Texas Health and Safety Code; or
Confined in jail, but otherwise eligible.
