Glasscock County, Texas
is known for its wide-open West Texas landscapes and its significant role in the Permian Basin, one of the nation's most productive energy regions. Centered around the community of Garden City, the county reflects a spirit of hard work, independence, and resilience. From its ranching heritage to its contributions to modern energy production, Glasscock County offers a unique blend of tradition, industry, and small-town character.
