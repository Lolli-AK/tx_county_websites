HARTLEY COUNTY EMPLOYEE OF THE YEAR 2025 "RONNIE GORDON "

MAY 1, 2026

Retired Hartley County Judge Ronnie Gordon passed on the gavel to the newly appointed Hartley County Judge,
The Honorable Camilla Green.

Camilla won the Republican primary election on March 3 and was appointed by the Commissioners' Court to fill the unexpired term of Judge Gordon.

The general election will be held on November 3, and the Co Judge's regular term will begin January 1, 2027.

Congratulations Judge Green!
