Information, Links, and Resources

Quick Access to Online Services

Hartley County, Texas is known for its sweeping High Plains landscapes, deep ranching heritage, and its connection to the legendary XIT Ranch, one of the largest ranches in American history. Centered around the community of Channing, the county reflects generations of agricultural tradition, western values, and a strong sense of independence. From its expansive prairie vistas to its enduring ranching legacy, Hartley County offers a distinctive blend of history, hard work, and the timeless character of the Texas Panhandle.

Hartley County Judge, The Honorable Camilla Green

MAY 1, 2026

Retired Hartley County Judge Ronnie Gordon passed on the gavel to the newly appointed Hartley County Judge, The Honorable Camilla Green. Camilla won the Republican primary election on March 3 and was appointed by the Commissioners' Court to fill the unexpired term of Judge Gordon.

The general election will be held on November 3, and the County Judge's regular term will begin January 1, 2027.

Congratulations Judge Green!
