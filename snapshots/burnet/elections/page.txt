The elections administrator is responsible for voter registration activities and election operations of Burnet County, Texas.  Burnet County has 21 approved voting precincts and approximately 40,200 registered voters.  If requested by a city, school district, or other political subdivision in Burnet County, the elections administrator will enter into a contract to conduct its elections.
Reasonable efforts are made to keep the information in this website current.  However, you are encouraged shortly before early voting starts for any election to revisit this site to confirm the location of early voting places and Election Day polling locations.  Do not hesitate to call the elections administrator with your questions.  I want you to vote!

Burnet County has County Wide Polling Locations!
Burnet County Voters can vote at any of the open polling locations around the county on election day.  Go to our current election page link at the top of this page to view information about the elections that are coming up! We welcome public comments!  Please email any comments to:
elections@burnetcountytexas.org
NO MORE STRAIGHT PARTY VOTING!
“House Bill 25, passed during the 85th Legislative Session, eliminated straight party voting effective September 1, 2020 (Section 64.004, Texas Election Code). If you wish to vote for all of the candidates affiliated with one party, you should select each candidate one at a time on your ballot.”
“El Proyecto de Ley 25 de la Cámara de Representantes, aprobado durante la 85a Sesión Legislativa, eliminó la votación de partido único a partir del 1 de septiembre de 2020 (Sección 64.004, Código Electoral de Texas). Si desea votar por todos los candidatos afiliados a un partido, debe seleccionar a cada candidato uno a la vez en su boleta.”
