Notice of Voting Order Priority
ELECTION DAY VOTE CENTERS!
Burnet County has County Wide Polling Locations  known as Vote Centers.  We are pleased to announce that as a registered voter in Burnet County, you may vote on election day at the nearest polling location that is open no matter where you are in the County.  You do not have to vote at your home precinct.  See the list below which contains all 19 polling locations which are all Vote Centers.
Polling locations change depending on the type of election. Locations for the May 26, 2026 Primary Elections are now posted below. As elections are ordered by cities and school districts, the election day vote centers that will be open are subject to change! We will post the final version of this page in mid-April!
Please call us if you have questions! 512-715-5288
