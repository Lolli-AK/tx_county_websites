2026 Williamson County Elections Sticker Design Contests
Bring Your Creativity to the Polls!
Williamson County Elections invites local students to submit their original artwork for the 2026 Sticker Design Contests. Winning designs will be printed and distributed at Williamson County polling locations for the November 3, 2026 Election.
Students can participate in one of two contests based on their grade level: the “Future Voter” Sticker Design Contest for elementary school students and the “I Voted” Sticker Design Contest for middle and high school students.
“Future Voter” Sticker Design Contest
Williamson County Elections is excited to announce its first-ever “Future Voter” Sticker Design Contest!
Who Can Enter?
The contest is open to elementary school students in grades K–5 who are currently enrolled in Williamson County.
Design Requirements
Designs may be created digitally or by hand.
Designs must be circular.
Designs must include the words “Future Voter.”
Entries must follow all contest rules and regulations.
The winning design will be printed and distributed at Williamson County polling locations for the November 3, 2026 Election.
“I Voted” Sticker Design Contest
Williamson County Elections is excited to announce its second “I Voted” Sticker Design Contest!
Who Can Enter?
The contest is open to middle and high school students in grades 6–12 who are currently enrolled in a Williamson County school.
Two Winners Will Be Selected
Two winning designs will be chosen:
One Middle School Winner
One High School Winner
The selected designs will become official Williamson County “I Voted” stickers for the upcoming election and will be distributed at polling locations.
Design Requirements
Designs may be created digitally or by hand.
Designs must be circular.
Designs must include the words “I Voted.”
Entries must follow all contest rules and regulations.
Contest Deadlines & Important Dates
August 7, 2026
Design submissions open.
September 4, 2026
Last day to submit a design. Entries must be received by 5:00 p.m.
September 8, 2026
A panel of judges will select the winners.
October 19, 2026
Early Voting begins! Look for the limited-edition winning “Future Voter” and “I Voted” stickers at the polls.
November 3, 2026
Election Day.
