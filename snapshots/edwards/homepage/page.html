Edwards County, Texas is defined by its rugged Hill Country landscapes, deep ranching roots, and strong sense of community. Located on the Edwards Plateau, the county is known for its expansive open spaces, abundant wildlife, and longstanding role in ranching and mohair production—centered in Rocksprings, often recognized as the “Mohair Capital of the World.” With a heritage shaped by land stewardship and a connection to the natural environment, Edwards County offers a quiet, rural lifestyle where tradition, resilience, and the outdoors continue to define its character.
