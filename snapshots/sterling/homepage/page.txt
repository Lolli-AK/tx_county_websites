Welcome to Sterling County—where West Texas heritage meets strong community values, open spaces, and a commitment to service, progress, and preserving our proud rural traditions.
Information, Links, and Resources
Quick Access to Online Services
Located in the heart of West Texas, Sterling County offers a welcoming rural atmosphere rooted in tradition and community pride.
Known for its wide-open landscapes and friendly residents, the county provides a peaceful lifestyle while supporting agriculture and local enterprise. With a strong sense of heritage and neighborly spirit, Sterling County is a place where simplicity and connection truly thrive.
