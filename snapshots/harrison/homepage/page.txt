Harrison County
, located in East Texas, is centered around the historic city of Marshall. Known for its rich heritage, the county features landmarks like the historic Harrison County Courthouse and attractions such as the Michelson Museum of Art. Each year, downtown Marshall comes alive during the Wonderland of Lights, drawing visitors from across the region. Nearby Caddo Lake State Park offers scenic outdoor recreation with its distinctive cypress-lined waterways.
