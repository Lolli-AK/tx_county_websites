Ochiltree County, Texas
is known for its rich agricultural heritage, expansive High Plains landscapes, and its place in the heart of the northern Texas Panhandle. Anchored by the City of Perryton, the county has grown from its early ranching roots into a thriving agricultural center, with wheat production playing a defining role in its history. From the scenic surroundings of Wolf Creek and Lake Fryer to its longstanding traditions of farming, ranching, and energy production, Ochiltree County reflects a distinctive blend of natural beauty, economic strength, and the hardworking character of the Texas Panhandle.
