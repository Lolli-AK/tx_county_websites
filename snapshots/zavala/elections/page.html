TextMyGov Announcement

The Zavala County Clerks Office is thrilled to introduce TextMyGov, a convenient and smart text messaging service! This service will enable residents to effortlessly receive important notifications and easily find specific information by texting the Zavala County Elections.

The Zavala County Clerks Office will officially launch the new text messaging system to the public on 02/16/2026. Residents may receive a text message from 80946 from the Zavala County Elections initiating the service. You can choose to opt-out at any time by texting STOP .

If you don’t receive the initial message, you can choose to opt-in by texting the keyword “ Zavala Co Elections ” to the number 80946 . After sending the initial keyword, you will be asked to reply “ YES ” to confirm opt-in.

Also starting soon, residents will be able to receive on-demand information 24/7 by texting 1-2 keywords to the Zavala’s TextMyGov number, ( 830) 448-0433 . You’ll be able to get quick information about Zavala County Elections .

The Zavala County Clerks Office use of this technology is another example of how our community enhances resident services by implementing 21 st -century solutions. We’re excited about this new way of communicating with residents and look forward to sharing important Zavala County Elections notifications. Other governmental entities across the country are utilizing this system with great success. Stay informed, stay ahead.

Sincerely,

Michelle B. Urrabazo

Zavala County Clerk
