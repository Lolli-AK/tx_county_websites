Welcome to Gonzales County, Texas

Where History Meets Opportunity

Step into Gonzales County, the proud birthplace of Texas Independence - where rich history meets modern opportunity. Perfectly positioned near San Antonio, Austin, and Victoria, our county offers unmatched access to big-city amenities while maintaining the charm, affordability, and friendliness of true small-town Texas.

Whether you're looking to start a business, build a home, or raise a family, Gonzales County delivers. With a lower cost of living, a growing economy, and the genuine southern hospitality found in communities like Gonzales, Nixon, Waelder, and beyond, it’s easy to see why more people are choosing to live, work, and invest here.

Come experience the spirit of Gonzales County — and be part of something legendary.
