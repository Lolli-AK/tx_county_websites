Yoakum County, Texas, is a proud West Texas community rooted strong in oil and gas production and agriculture. With its wide-open spaces, hardworking spirit, and close-knit towns like Plains and Denver City, the county offers a welcoming atmosphere and a high quality of life. Yoakum County values its heritage while embracing steady growth, making it a dependable and resilient place to live, work, and do business.

News and Announcements

The Commissioner's Court of Yoakum County meets every Monday at 10:00 A.M. at the Yoakum County Courthouse. If Monday is a holiday, the Court will traditionally meet the following business day at 10:00 A.M. All meetings of the Yoakum County Commissioner's Court will be considered Regular Meetings.
