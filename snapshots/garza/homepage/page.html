Community Supervision and Corrections Week
July 20–26, 2025

Each year across the State of Texas, we dedicate a week to recognize the professionals who serve in Pretrial, Probation, and Parole Supervision and the vital work they perform. The week of July 20–26, 2025, provides an opportunity to honor those who devote countless hours to enhancing public safety while making a positive difference in the lives of individuals under supervision, their families, victims, and the communities they serve.
