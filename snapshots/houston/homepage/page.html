Welcome to Houston County, Texas

BURN BAN IN EFFECT

Houston County is served by US Highway 287 and State Highways 7, 19 and 21. All of these highways intersect at the Courthouse Square in downtown Crockett. SH 21 follows the 300-year-old route of the El Camino Real de los Tejas National Historic Trail. As of the 2010 census , the population was 23,732. [1] Its county seat is Crockett . [2] The county is named for Sam Houston , a president of the Republic of Texas and Governor of Texas.

**The Courthouse has ONE ENTRANCE ONLY now, through the basement door on the WEST Side.
