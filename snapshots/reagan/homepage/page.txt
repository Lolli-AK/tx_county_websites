Where History Continues to Shape the Future.
Reagan County, Texas is known for its pioneering place in West Texas history and the discovery that helped transform the region for generations. In 1923, Santa Rita No. 1 struck oil west of Big Lake, opening the Permian Basin to oil production and beginning a legacy that would leave a lasting mark on both Texas and the University of Texas. Anchored by the City of Big Lake and surrounded by expansive West Texas landscapes, Reagan County reflects a distinctive blend of ranching heritage, energy innovation, and the independent spirit that continues to shape the region today.
